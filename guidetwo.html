Which Tress Are Protected in Los Angeles?

According to the Los angeles Tree Protection Ordinance The following trees are protected:

  • • All deciduous trees, except for Fruit trees and walnut trees
  • • The Scots pine (all other conifers are not protected)

this meaans:

  • ✅ Oaks, beeches, maples, linden trees, chestnuts & Co. are protected and may not be felled without official permission.
  • ✅ Fruit trees (e.g. B. Apple, cherry, pear) and walnut trees are exempt from the statutes and can usually be removed without permission.
  • ✅ Conifers other than Scots pine do not fall under the special protection of the Tree Protection Ordinance, but are subject to other legal regulations.

What other Rules apply to tree felling?

  • ✅ Felling ban during the protection period: From 1. March to 30 September applies according to Federal Nature Conservation Act (§ 39) a general ban on felling to protect breeding birds and other animals. Exceptions are only possible with official approval.
  • ✅ Danger trees: If a tree has a immediate danger for people or buildings (e.g. B. due to risk of breakage or disease), a immediate precipitation be necessary. In most cases, however, a report or expert opinion is required.
