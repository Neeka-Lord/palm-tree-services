about us

our phillosophy

Tree care is more than a profession - it is responsibility, craft, Technology and a contribution to public space. palms & tree care services works people who have this responsibility take seriously. The company was founded in 2025, supported by two Managing directors who together have over two decades of practical experience in the field of height work, rope climbing technology and tree care look back.

Our company was not born out of a plan on paper, but from lived experience on construction sites, in parks, on roadsides, in cemeteries, in gardens. What we have seen again and again: technical questionable measures, unnecessarily radical interventions, lack of Understanding of the living creature tree. Our founding is a reaction - and a statement: There are other ways. Better. And with attitude.

Our claim is not the largest machinery or the loudest appearance. Our aim is quality. And this through well-founded Expertise, precise implementation and the awareness that each measure has consequences - for the tree, for its surroundings, for the people who live for him.
We do not see our work as a pure service, but as Interface between ecology, technology and social space. Just now in urban context, where habitats are compacted and trees are under If you are under stress, you need a high level of sensitivity. It It is not enough to read a tree - you also have to understand how it into its environment: traffic-related, aesthetic, biological.

We work precisely, reliably and on an equal footing - with the trees and with the people who commission us. Modern rope access technology and current standards are self-evident to us, the decisive factor remains but always look at the whole thing: Where is the tree? What does he need? And how can this be reconciled with the requirements of its environment bring?

It's not about big words or spectacular interventions. It is about good craftsmanship, clear decisions and knowing when to intervene is necessary - and when not. Those who work with us do not receive empty promises, but a honest assessment, reliable implementation and the good feeling, that it is not just „anything“ that is done - but the right thing.

tree care with responsibility - our phillosophy and fundation

Palm & Tree Care service was founded in February 2015 - based on over 25 years of practical experience in professional tree care. Denis j Romero has been working in the industry since 2010, Nancy Brenner since 2015. Both managing directors were previously active throughout Los Angeles as sole proprietors, trainers and subcontractors - in maintenance, special felling and rope-assisted height access.

Denis J Romero - Managing Director, arborist, climbing professional, instructor

Denis came to tree care via sport climbing - and found a field of work in which technique, understanding of nature and precision craftsmanship intertwine. There are few areas of work in tree care that Henry doesn't know firsthand.

What began as a fascination with climbing technique developed over the years into broad professional expertise. Denis is familiar with maintenance, felling, special contracts in inner-city areas as well as demanding industrial climbing projects. Whether routine or special assignment - Denis works focused, solution-oriented and with a sure sense of the conditions on site. As an instructor, he shares his knowledge with great joy in his job, conveys technical connections vividly and motivatively - and infects others with his enthusiasm.

Miguelon Garcia - Managing Director, arborist, organizational talent, nature person

Although he grew up in Englewood, he had a close connection to nature from the very beginning. Early on, he developed an interest in woody plants, fruit tree pruning, and the cultivation of fruit and vegetables. Tree care was initiated through projects in the field of forest conversion and new forest planting.

Particular attention is paid to urban space - where safety requirements, technical constraints and ecological aspects meet. These contrasts shape his approach and his commitment to implementing tree care in urban areas thoughtfully, responsibly and with a clear eye for the whole.
