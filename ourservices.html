efficient tree felling techniques for every challenge

We are your specialist for tree felling in Berlin and Brandenburg. Whether with rope climbing technique (SKT) or aerial work platforms - we offer safe and efficient execution. Our team of experienced tree climbers will ensure the professional removal of your trees, regardless of their location or size.

tree feling with aerial work platforms-efficient and safe

Where space permits, we use aerial work platforms. This method allows for rapid and low-risk tree felling, especially in freestanding trees or on extensive plots. The aerial work platform offers the advantage that our team can work efficiently without having to use complex climbing techniques. This will save you time and benefit from a smooth process with the highest safety standards. This technique is particularly suitable for large-scale plants or properties with sufficient space to ensure rapid removal.

rope climbing techniques-Maximun flexibility in tree felling

The rope climbing technique is particularly suitable for difficult-to-access or critical tree sites. Our specialized tree climbers work with professional rope safety systems to selectively remove branches and trunk parts and abseil them in a controlled manner. This is particularly advantageous in narrow backyards, on roadsides or in densely built-up areas where classic felling is not possible. In addition, this method allows for a gentle approach, which keeps surrounding vegetation protected. Thanks to the rope climbing technique, we can work precisely and at the same time respond flexibly to the local conditions.

We offer short-term hazard removal and remove damaged or uprooted trees quickly and safely. Our experts assess the situation on site and choose the safest method for removing the tree to prevent further damage. Through special indulgence techniques, we ensure that no additional dangers arise even in sensitive areas.

consulting & disposal

tree felling - individual advice and legal aspects

individual advice and assessment

Each tree requires an individual approach. That's why we offer an on-site assessment. In many cases, a photo assessment is sufficient for a quote; in more complex cases, a detailed inspection is required. We examine the stability of the tree, its environment and possible risk factors. Based on this analysis, we will determine the best course of action for your tree felling and advise you on possible alternatives such as crown shortening or a tree conservation measure. Especially for larger plants or trees in sensitive areas, a well-founded assessment is crucial for safe and efficient implementation.

sustainability and profesional disposal

Tree felling should always be considered as a last resort. Therefore, we are examining in advance whether alternatives such as crown reductions or stabilization systems could be a solution. If felling is unavoidable, we take care of the environmentally sound disposal of the wood and branches. Log wood can be sawn and further processed or used as firewood. Branches and other clippings are properly removed and disposed of. In addition, we will be happy to advise you on possible replacement plantings to compensate for the ecological value of the removed tree. In certain cases, the wood can also be used for ecological purposes, such as deadwood habitats in green spaces. Our aim is to minimize environmental impacts through targeted measures.

legal regulations, tree protection statutes and felling permits

In california, tree felling is regulated by the Tree Protection Act. As a rule, they may only be carried out between October and February. Outside this period, a special permit is required if the tree is not at acute risk of breakage. We will be happy to advise you on the applicable regulations and assist you in applying for the necessary permits. For listed trees or green spaces, additional evidence is often required, which we can coordinate for you. We will take care of the full processing with the relevant authorities for you, so you don't have to worry about anything.

safety and labout standards

Our work is always carried out according to the highest safety standards and in compliance with all relevant regulations. Our teams are specially trained and equipped with modern technology to carry out tree felling efficiently and with little risk. Through regular training and the use of tested rope and safety technology, we guarantee you that all work will be carried out professionally and safely.

In private gardens and on the grounds of residential complexes, targeted tree care also ensures vital trees, ensures road safety and creates a harmonious overall picture. Whether for property management, homeowners associations or private customers - we offer professional pruning measures for healthy, stable and well-maintained trees in The City of Angels..

Our tree pruning services for los angeles city and sorrounding areas

Depending on the tree species and location, we offer targeted care measures:

  • Crown care - removal of bad growth and dead wood to promote natural development
  • Crown shortening - reduction of the treetop to stabilize and reduce wind load
  • Clearance profile section - Ensuring clear walking and driving paths in urban areas
  • Building clearance - Protection of buildings and utility lines from overgrowth
  • Deadwood removal - removal of branches at risk of breakage to minimize accident risks
  • Topiary & maintenance pruning - Optimizing tree shape to promote growth and appearance
  • Structural pruning for young trees - supporting a healthy crown structure from the start

why us tree pruning important?

For trees to thrive optimally, they need regular care. Professional tree pruning has the following advantages:

  • Controlled growth - regulating tree growth to promote a stable structure
  • Road safety - reducing risks from falling branches
  • Protection of buildings & infrastructure - prevention of damage to facades, roofs and paths
  • Improved lighting conditions - More sunlight for adjacent plants and buildings
  • Healthy development - removing diseased or dead branches to maintain vitality
  • Prevention of diseases - Reduction of the risk of fungal or pest infestation through targeted cutting
