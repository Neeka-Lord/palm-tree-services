our philosophy

Precision and Craftsmanship - Our working methods and demands

We do not see our work as a pure service, but as an interface between ecology, technology and social space. Especially in an urban context, where habitats are densified and trees are under stress, a high degree of tact is required. It's not enough to read a tree - you also have to understand how it fits into its surroundings: in terms of traffic, aesthetics, biology.
We work precisely, reliably and at eye level - with the trees and with the people who commission us. Modern rope access technology and current standards are a matter of course for us, but it is always crucial to look at the big picture: Where is the tree? What does he need? frequently asked questions:

can i just cut down my tree?

NO. In los angeles city and county many trees are protected by a tree protection regulations. a permit is required, especially for deciduous trees, oaks, beaches and other protected species.

Costs vary depending on the tree species, size, location and required felling method. a free on-site inspection is necessary to determine and exact price.

what is the tree protection ordenance in the city of Los Angeles, CA?

The Los Angeles trees protection ordinance protects certain trees, such as deiduous trees and scots pines with a trunk circumference of 80cm or more. felling is only permitted between octuber and february to protect the bird's nesting season. violations can be punished with heavy fines.

more about it ➜
is there a LA county tree protection statute?

Yes, there is not statewide tree protection ordinance in LA county, but there are municipal regulations. The provisions vary according to the county or municipality. Many trees, especially in urban areas, require a permit before they can be felled.

more about it ➜
do i have to plant a tree if i cut down a tree?

In many cases, replacement planting is necessary when a protected trees is felled espeically in Los Angeles city beaches and mountain counties. tree protection regulations often require that a new plant be planted as compensation unless the tree has not been protected or has been removed due to acute danger.

more about it ➜
what pruning measures are permitted outside of the felling season?

During the felling barrier, maintenance and preservation cuts, crown care, clearance profile cuts and light topiary cuts on hedges and shrub are permitted in Los Angeles city and county. however, severe cuts or radical pruning are prohibted to protect the vitality of the trees.
